## Performance

Before this pass there was **one** open performance issue: #77 (bigger worlds: batching and machine sleep; measured 15 ms a frame and ~3,200 draw calls for the 40-machine gallery). #73 and #74 are closed.

Filed now:
- #188 Physics spikes of 60–70 ms in ground worlds. Measured here, headless at 1× with `HEROIC_FPS_REPORT=1`: lonely-rover-opening typically 3–6 ms physics with samples of 20.6, 21.8 and 62.7 ms; trench 2–5 ms with 15.5 and 70.7 ms. A 120 Hz tick has 8.3 ms. Suspect (unconfirmed): `TerrainView.Reshape` rebuilds the whole ground mesh and replaces the whole height-map collision body on every ground change, up to 5 times a second. Target: no sample over 16.7 ms in 120 s of each world.
- #187 Jolt's 47.1 rad/s spin cap (engine default). Filed under 2 Physics components because it is an engine limit, not frame time: it blocks #64's ~1,500 rpm generator, and raising it to 600 broke the belt-drive and catapulta tests (4dcdc96). Its performance side: whatever fix is chosen has to report the gallery's physics ms before and after.

Not filed: many machines in one world is #77 already. The terrain rebuild's per-frame costs quoted in TerrainView.cs comments were fixed in 43a5447; #188 is the measured remainder.
