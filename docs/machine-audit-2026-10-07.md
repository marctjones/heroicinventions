# Machine audit before v0.2.0-alpha (2026-10-07)

Read-only on game and engine code. Branch `worktree-agent-a795ded75d2042d4e`, game at `2dcb1f2`. Nothing here was changed in the machines; this file is the only addition.

## How each machine was checked

1. Coverage: which test in `racket/heroic/tests/machine-behavior-test.rkt` (sim side `simulate`, Jolt side `godot-simulate`), or `tests/HeroicInventions.Sim.Tests`, checks the machine's function against a number. A test that only checks thresholds (`> 100 rpm`, `x < -15`) is marked **weak**. A machine that only has a test when placed in a world is marked **world**. **N** means no test.
2. Every one of the 95 machines was run in the real game, headless: `HEROIC_AUTORUN=1 HEROIC_AUTOSELECT=<name> HEROIC_DEBUG_PHYSICS=1 HEROIC_AUDIT=1`, at 1x to 20x speed, 4 s to 5,000 s of sim time. The numbers in the "what it did" column come from that trace, or, where the HUD prints no per-part values (jet wheels, enclosures, hearths, airlock, glass rooms and others), from `HEROIC_TRACE` runs of the same machine.
3. Every machine was also run in the real window with `tools/gui-check.sh` (default camera, 1280x800, a few hundred frames in) and its frame looked at. That covers a part missing, jammed, through the floor or flying off, but only as far as the default camera sees it (see Framing below).
4. For the machines whose test is weak or absent I worked the number out myself from the header (or from the part's own formula) and compared. Where the header has no worked number there is nothing to compare with and the verdict is UNCLEAR.
5. The in-game description text was read for the machines where the trace disagreed with it.

A verdict of OK means: the trace (or the strong test, whose numbers I re-checked against the game run) matches the number the header works out, and the frame shows nothing wrong. It does not mean every claim in the header was re-derived; where only a test carries a number the row says so.

## WRONG

**buried-crate: falls through the floor when picked from the menu.** Run alone, the crate (placed 1 m under where the ground should be) has no ground: `y = -492.24 m` at 10 s, 98 m/s and still accelerating; the audit prints `OVERLAP @StaticBody3D@6 <-> crate start 1000 mm inside each other` at the first tick; the HUD shows `137 m/s` and the frame shows the crate gone. It only works in `game/worlds/dig-out.world`, where the test "Digging out (#54)" checks 14.2 kN and the fourth spit. The menu lists it as a plain `buried-crate` with no description. `trench-crew`, `cistern-drain`, `spill-tank` and `hillside-pond` have the same dependence on a map but stand still or say so; this one is the only one that visibly breaks.

## UNCLEAR

- **bellows-forge**: the burn-rate claims are checked by a strong test and pass. But in the game the two crucibles (sealed 50 g bronze water boilers with no pressure rating) read `1,242.7 C, 1,115,121 kPa` (bare) and `4,923 C, 8,215,851 kPa` (blown) at 400 s, and neither bursts nor melts (bronze melts near 950 C). The header makes no claim about the crucible, so I cannot call it wrong; it looks like it wants an open crucible or a rating.
- **crane-hoist**: header says alone nothing turns the drum. The stone sits (0.01 m) but the drum free-spins, 7.6 rpm at 0.5 s decaying to 1.1 rpm at 10 s, 240 deg turned. The stone starts 5 mm above the ground with the rope just taut; a 5 mm drop is enough to spin the drum (about 12 rpm by hand). Harmless.
- **roman-crane**: stone rose 1.99 m in 30 s while the wheel speeds up from 0.5 to 5.0 rpm, past the header's 3 rpm. This is known issue #86; listed for completeness, not counted as new.
- **torsion-catapult**, **vitruvian-catapulta**: the headers give no worked number (the spring stiffnesses are stated estimates), and their tests only check thresholds, so there is nothing to compare with. Behaviour is plausible (onager lands near 20 m then rolls to 31.1 m; bolt rests at 45.7 m). The catapulta's in-game description says it flies at ~25 m/s and lands ~11 m away; the trace shows ~18 m/s at 0.5 s and the bolt on the floor (`y = 0.01`) from 1 s, sliding 46 m to rest. The shot is level with the floor, so it never flies. The text and the behaviour disagree.

## Other findings (not machine verdicts)

- **HEROIC_AUDIT crashes when a machine spawns bodies.** `MachineView.Audit.cs:30` (`_audit[b]` in `AuditTick`) throws `KeyNotFoundException` every physics tick once `battering-rams` breaks a post (the pieces are new bodies, not in the table). The run wrote 27 MB of errors, never reached `HEROIC_QUIT_AFTER_SIM_SECONDS` and had to be killed (exit 124 at 600 s). Without `HEROIC_AUDIT` the machine runs normally; I used that run for its numbers.
- **No camera profile for 18 machines**, so the menu opens them with the default camera at (0, 1, 2): `boulder`, `buried-crate`, `cargo-crate`, `cistern`, `cistern-and-trough`, `cistern-drain`, `crane-hoist`, `crate`, `dry-mill`, `fall-and-swing`, `falling-stones`, `field-windmill`, `free-sails`, `hillside-pond`, `spill-tank`, `trench-crew`, `trough`, `walkers-wheel`. In the frames: `falling-stones` shows no ball at all (they fall 350 m, the frame shows only the landing ring), `walkers-wheel`, `crane-hoist`, `free-sails` and `field-windmill` show a close-up of one member, `fall-and-swing` is a few pixels. Most of these are meant to be placed in a world, but all appear on the menu.
- **Framing at 1280x800** (the size gui-check runs at): `trebuchet`, `torsion-catapult` and `vitruvian-catapulta` show the machine at the right edge under the info panel (or not at all) after the shot; `dam-break` shows only the race; `ball-ramp` and `carts` lose their balls and carts off the edge within seconds (the balls roll on without end, 222 m at 100 s, 2.25 m/s).
- **Header error, machine right:** `falling-stones.rkt` says the iron ball has 47 m/s at 6 s. `v = vt tanh(g t / vt)` with its own `vt = 133.4` gives 55.3 m/s (the 58.9 bare-fall figure in the same line is right). The game matches the formula.
- **Stale in-game text:** the trebuchet description says "flies ~16 m forward"; the header and trace say 13.1 m (the 16 m was the pre-#80 figure).

Known issues, not re-reported: #138 (rope on a damped lever under-holds its load), #80 (trebuchet second snap), #86 (Roman crane speed mismatch and framing).
