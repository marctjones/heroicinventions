# "Build five machines": a mouse-only tutorial (plan, 2026-10-12)

Owner's request: "a tutorial that guides me step by step through me using a mouse to build a series of 5 machines starting with a simple machine with 3-5 parts up until 2 or 3 complex machines with 10+ parts." Designed by Fable from main 88a4135 (line numbers there). Owner decisions: the series goes in the Lessons menu AFTER today's four (see-saw, pulley, ramp, water) under a "Build five machines" heading; lesson 4 keeps all four stages (256:1) with the millstones' load set by the lesson (`#:load` on the sails). Owner rules: allow don't forbid; physics real (every Test checks a traced fact against a number worked BEFORE, in the lesson header, as game/lessons/02-pulley.lesson does, and the header cites the trace); legibility; show state in the scene.

Built in three slices, each landed with the full suite: A = format gaps + lesson 05; B = lessons 06-07; C = lessons 08-09.

## Constraints from the code
- Lessons are data: src/HeroicInventions.Sim/Editor/Lessons.cs:7-52 (step fields), check kinds place/join/test and outcomes balanced/lifted/rolled/level (:135-152); `then` substitutes only {id} (:363) and is ignored on join steps (:426). Run by game/scripts/BuildMode.Lessons.cs; Tests by BuildMode.Test.cs / TestReport.cs (a Test runs until quiet or 30 s, TestReport.cs:70-100: a turning machine always runs the full 30 s). TestFacts: rises, WheelTurn(Turns, EndRpm) (TestReport.cs:24-30), tanks, ropes; nothing electric.
- Jolt clamps bodies at 314.159 rad/s = 3,000 rpm (MachineView.SpinLimit.cs). A cranked train (wheel with #:drive-rpm) runs its driven gears on unlimited motors: its speed ratio is real, its torque not (MachineView.Gears.cs:11-19); a driven wheel's own drive-torque cap is real (MachineView.Drives.cs:21-31).
- The workshop is Earth air, 20 C: the palette's 10 m 1,500 kg sails in 6 m/s give tau* = 8,171 N.m (racket/machines/windmills.rkt), free 3.0 rad/s; unloaded 256:1 puts the last pinion past the clamp.
- Not mouse-buildable today: a water wheel (needs inflow + channel onto the wheel), the Roman crane's jib (machine-authored).

## The five lessons (worked numbers to be TRACED and cited in each header before the file is final)
**05-gears "Crank and gears" (4 parts).** 1 m post; a 72 on top made a crank (then `#:drive-rpm 30`, `#:drive-torque 5`); "Add a meshing gear" 18; on the 18, "Add a meshing gear" 36. Module 5: pitch radii 0.18/0.045/0.09; centres 0.225 and 0.135. Crank 30 rpm -> 18 at 120 rpm (other way) -> 36 at 60 rpm (crank's way); 72:36 = 2:1, the middle gear only passes it on. Test (30 s): the 36's EndRpm 60 +- 2 (and the 18's 120). Steps: post (target at 0 0 0); gear 72 on top (target at 0 1.19 0 within 0.3, check place teeth 72 as crank, then drive lines); select crank, highlight the "Add a meshing gear" button, variant m5-18, check mesh (with crank) (teeth 18) as second; same for the 36 on second; Test: check turned (part third) (rpm 60) (within 2); done text quotes {second.rpm} {third.rpm}.
**06-windlass "Windlass" (5 parts + axle, rope).** 3 m post (preset); drum-15cm on top (target at 0 3.15 0 within 0.25); "Put a wheel on this shaft" on the drum: a 1.2 m cart wheel, then drive-rpm 3, drive-torque 40; a granite 10 cm block on the ground under the drum; rope drum -> block, the lesson re-ties it wound on the drum and hangs the block 0.3 m up. Numbers: block 2.7 kg, 26.5 N, 3.97 N.m on the drum, rim needs 6.6 N: R/r = 4; 3 rpm = 0.3142 rad/s, rope winds 4.71 cm/s, 1.414 m in 30 s. Test: lifted (part load) (about 1.41) (within 0.15).
**07-crane "Treadwheel crane" (7 build steps).** treadwheel-4.0m oak (target at 0 2.4 0 within 2.5; then drive-rpm 1, drive-torque 1373: two walkers); drum-30cm on its shaft ("Put a wheel on this shaft"), 1.0 m behind; jib post 3 m at (1.5 0 1.0); pulley-20cm bronze on it; a 0.5 m granite stone at (1.7 0.25 1.0) (then size 0.5); rope drum -> stone over the pulley, wound on the drum, stone hung 0.3 m up. Numbers: two walkers 2 x 70 x 9.81 x 2.0 x sin 30 = 1,373 N.m; stone 337.5 kg, 3,311 N, x 0.3 = 993 N.m; surplus spins the wheel to 1 rpm in 0.33 s; hoist 3.14 cm/s, 0.94 m in 30 s. One walker (687 N.m) loses: the fail text says so. Test: lifted (part stone) (about 0.94) (within 0.12).
**08-mill "The mill" (10 parts).** 10.6 m post (preset) at (0.45 0 -1.45); windmill on its top (target at 0 11.05 0 within 0.3), then `#:load 11440` (the millstones); four "Add a stage" clicks (72 drives 18), roles wheel-a..pinion-e; the live label 256:1. Numbers: tau* 8,171, w* 1.5, I 50,000 + gears reflected 2,634 kg.m2, time constant 9.7 s; with load 11,440 steady 0.9 rad/s, pinion 230 rad/s = 2,200 rpm; at 30 s ~2,100 rpm. Test: turned (part pinion-e) (rpm 2100) (within 150); done quotes the traced turns ratio (256).
**09-generator "Mill to generator" (11 parts).** post + windmill (no load); three stages (64:1) as wheel-a..pinion-d; generator near the last pinion, then `#:on {pinion-d}`; heat store (cells) on the ground; battery bank beside it, then `#:in {cells}` and `(set {generator} #:charges {id})`. Numbers: generator torque cap 12 N.m (Electrics/Generator.cs:86), k = 12/(261.8-157.08) = 0.1146 N.m per rad/s; steady X = 189.2 rad/s = 1,807 rpm, 3.68 N.m, 557 W into the bank; at 30 s ~1,750 rpm, ~440 W. Bank charges only 0-45 C (store starts at 20 C). Test: charging (part generator) (watts 440) (within 120) and turned (part pinion-d) (rpm 1750) (within 120). Fail text: under 1,500 rpm nothing charges.

## Slice B as built (2026-10-17)
Lessons 06 and 07 traced as worked (each header cites its trace): the windlass's block rose 1.4107 m (worked 1.413), the crane's stone 0.9350 m (worked 0.935), one walker loses it to the ground (rise -0.3000, settled at 8.92 s). Where the build differs from the plan above, and why:
- Gap 7 done: "Put a gear on this shaft" has a `Which:` list (every catalogue wheel) and names its pick ("Put a wheel on this shaft"); a step's `(variant ...)` with `button:gear_on` picks it. The default spot clears a wide wheel already on the axle (`ShaftSlots.ClearT`), so the cart wheel starts 0.386 m behind the drum and the drum 1.25 m behind the treadwheel (the plan's "1.0 m behind" put the 1.2 m drum inside the 1.2 m wheel). Catalogue cart and disc wheels were built as fixtures (`CatalogueEntry.WheelKinds` was behind shape.rkt's); now they are wheels, so the windlass's handle turns.
- Gap 3 done: `(wind-on ROLE) (hang H) [(over ROLE)]`, joins of kind arbor and mesh, then-lines on joins. Gap 4's `{ROLE}` in then-lines was needed now: the engine drives an axle by its first wheel, and the shaft button makes the drum first, so the windlass's hand is `(set {drum} #:drive-rpm 3)` on the wheel's step (on the wheel itself it would turn nothing).
- A step's `(preset "3 m")` (the card's size button pressed, so the green target is the 3 m post); lesson 05 says `(preset "1 m")` so it stays right after lesson 06.
- Windlass: the 3 m post made 15 cm square and the drum 3.22 m up (its 0.21 m flanges clear the post's top): the block hangs under the drum's winding side at x = 0.15, and a 30 cm post would hold it off. The view lifted so the drum sits under the lesson panel.
- Crane: the jib post 4 m out at x = -4 (a post inside the treadwheel's pick box, which spans the wheel's axle to the drum, can't be clicked on top), the pulley overhanging its outer face so the 0.5 m stone hangs clear of it; seen from behind the wheel at 35° to its axle (the rope nearest the camera, and the drum's slide along the axle readable by the mouse). The done text invites the one-walker Test (687 typed in place of 1373) rather than the lesson making it.
